  "summary": "The paper studies privacy-preserving activation steering for LLM alignment. It proposes PSA, which computes per-layer steering vectors from paired positive/negative demonstrations, clips and normalizes per-example activation differences, and adds Gaussian noise to obtain differentially private steering vectors. These vectors are then reused at inference time to steer arbitrary numbers of user queries, with the claim that privacy cost is paid once offline and subsequent generations are post-processing. Empirically, the paper evaluates PSA on seven alignment-related behavioral benchmarks across several open-source LLMs and compares against zero-shot, mean steering, and PCA steering. It also introduces a canary-based membership inference attack tailored to activation steering and reports reduced empirical leakage under PSA.",

    "Provides a clear DP framing and connects sensitivity control to clipping of per-example activation differences.",
