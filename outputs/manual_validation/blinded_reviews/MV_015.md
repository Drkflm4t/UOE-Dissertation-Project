The method supports both direct translation and post-hoc correction of GPT-3.5 outputs. A key contribution is a correction framework combining supervised fine-tuning on synthetically perturbed FOL examples with chain-of-thought-style intermediate corrections, followed by PPO-based RLHF using a verifier-derived reward based on a proposed logical equivalence metric mixed with BLEU. Experiments on FOLIO and LogicNLI show that direct LogicLLaMA outperforms GPT-3.5 and that the correction mode approaches GPT-4-level performance on FOLIO at lower cost.",
